How the Gioia method works

First-order concepts stay close to participants’ own words. Second-order themes interpret patterns across concepts. Aggregate dimensions express broader theoretical ideas when the evidence supports them. Trace every interpretation back to quotations and consider alternative readings.

Adapted from Gioia, Corley & Hamilton (2013). A data structure is one part of qualitative research; it does not establish theoretical saturation or a complete grounded theory.
