How scoring works

Each core habit receives 0–5: absent or violated, weak, mixed, competent, strong, or consistently excellent. Unobserved criteria are excluded from the provisional total. Scores describe your interviewing, not whether your idea is good.

Listening is assessed through follow-up quality, not a word-count target. Commitments are tracked separately; exploratory interviews do not require a purchase commitment.

Classroom rubric adapted from Rob Fitzpatrick’s The Mom Test; these are AI-assisted practice scores, not an official grading system.

Voice and scene

Dictation needs microphone permission and a supported browser on HTTPS or localhost. Stop dictation, review the text, then send. Your browser may send audio to its speech service; this page stores only the confirmed text. Natural AI voice uses the class speech service and incurs API usage. Browser voice is an optional device-based alternative. The stage uses the exact imported customer image. A plain studio background is blended into the scene locally, with a speaking indicator and expression label; the illustrated fallback supports approximate lip sync when no image is available.

The speech language also sets the customer’s reply language. Coaching follows the interview transcript.
