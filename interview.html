How scoring works

Each core habit receives 0–5: absent or violated, weak, mixed, competent, strong, or consistently excellent. Unobserved criteria are excluded from the provisional total. Scores describe your interviewing, not whether your idea is good.

Listening is assessed through follow-up quality, not a word-count target. Commitments are tracked separately; exploratory interviews do not require a purchase commitment.

Classroom rubric adapted from Rob Fitzpatrick’s The Mom Test; these are AI-assisted practice scores, not an official grading system.
