The contribution margin ladder

Pick one thing your venture sells and follow its price down the ladder. Each step takes away one kind of cost, so you can see how much of every sale you actually keep. Estimates are fine. Just note where each number came from.

1 Start with one sale

Count the materials, packaging, the labor for that one sale, and shipping or delivery. Haven’t launched yet? Use your best estimate.

Stuck on a number? Ask the AI for a starting estimate

Tell it what you already know. It suggests numbers for the blanks and explains each one. Nothing goes into your worksheet until you accept it, so check each suggestion against a real quote or price if you can.

2 What it costs to win the sale

Ads, flyers, a booth fee, a sales commission: anything you pay to get this customer to buy. If you pay a lump sum, spread it over the sales it brings in. If you pay a set amount for each sale, enter that amount.

Sales cost 1

Use the same stretch of time for both numbers, such as one month of ads and that month’s sales. No sales cost? Enter $0.

3 Fees and everything else

Card or platform fees, plus each sale’s share of costs such as rent, insurance or permits. If your stall costs $60 a month and you sell 100 bowls, that adds $0.60 to each bowl.

Enter 0 for anything that doesn’t apply, and count each cost only once.
